## 1. Scope, and why this distinction comes first

Camilo Andres Hurtado Carvajal operates FreezeHub, a service that lets organizations declare
and consult software deployment restrictions. In relation to the personal data it processes,
Camilo Andres Hurtado Carvajal acts in **two distinct capacities**, and which one applies
determines who the data subject should address.

### 1.1 As Controller

In relation to data collected for its own purposes:

- the contact details of anyone requesting a product demonstration;
- the registration details of anyone creating an account, before an organization exists;
- the billing identifiers associated with a contract;
- the service's operational logs.

Over this data Camilo Andres Hurtado Carvajal determines the purpose, answers the data subject
directly, and is who enquiries and complaints should be addressed to.

### 1.2 As Processor

In relation to the data a customer organization enters into the product — its own users'
accounts, its audit records, its deployment checks, its catalogues and integrations — **the
customer organization is the Controller** and Camilo Andres Hurtado Carvajal is the Processor.
That data is processed solely on that organization's instructions and is not used for any
purpose of our own.

**What this means for you in practice:** if you are a user of an organization that uses
FreezeHub, a request about your data must be addressed to that organization. If it reaches us,
we will forward it to them and tell you we have done so; we cannot unilaterally delete or
modify data for which we are not the Controller.

**The first purpose is the only one not founded on consent, and it is worth saying why.**
Article 10 of Ley 1581 de 2012 exempts data of a public nature from the consent requirement,
and article 3 of Decreto 1377 de 2013 counts among such data that relating to a person's trade
or profession. The name, role and organization of someone acting in a professional capacity,
published by that organization or by the person themselves, are processed on that basis.

**The exemption covers consent, not the duty to inform.** Article 12 continues to apply: the
first message identifies the Controller, states its address, links to this Policy and offers to
stop writing. The rights in section 4 are exercised in the same way, and a deletion request is
acted on immediately.

**Two limits declared rather than left to be discovered.** A work email address is the most
debatable part of this basis, so it is used solely to address the person in their professional
role and never to build profiles or for any other purpose. And this Policy governs processing
under Colombian law: it does not displace the rules of the recipient's own country, some of
which require prior consent to *send* commercial communications even where processing the data
is lawful here.

**No sensitive data is processed** within the meaning of article 5 of Ley 1581 de 2012, nor any
data concerning children or adolescents. The service is not directed at minors.

### 2.2 As Processor

Data entered by a customer organization is processed solely to provide the contracted service,
in accordance with that organization's instructions and the data transmission agreement entered
into with it. That organization determines the purpose and the retention period.

## 3. Consent

Processing requires the data subject's **prior, express and informed consent** (article 9 of
Ley 1581 de 2012), save in the cases set out in article 10. Consent is requested at the moment
of collection, and a record of it is retained — including the date and the exact text that was
presented — so that the data subject can request proof of the consent given, as article 8(b)
allows.

**The only purpose founded on article 10 is the commercial contact in section 2.1.** For that
purpose there is no consent to prove, so what is retained instead is a record of the public
source each item of data came from and the date it was obtained. This allows the data subject
to learn the origin of their data and exercise the rights in section 4, which is the interest
article 8(b) protects where consent does exist.

## 4. Rights of the data subject

Under article 8 of Ley 1581 de 2012, the data subject has the right to:

a) **Know, update and rectify** their personal data.
b) **Request proof of the consent** given, except where the law does not require it.
c) **Be informed**, on request, of the use made of their data.
d) **Lodge complaints** with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio for breaches of the
   law.
e) **Withdraw consent and/or request deletion** of the data, where no legal or contractual duty
   prevents it.
f) **Access their personal data free of charge.**

### 4.1 Two limits declared rather than left to be discovered

**Some data cannot be deleted.** Records with accounting and tax effect must be retained for
the period the law requires, and a deletion request does not override that duty. The data
subject will be told when this is the case.

**Backups lag behind deletion.** Deletion is carried out immediately in live systems. Existing
backups are destroyed when their retention period expires, and if one is restored, the deletion
is applied to it again.

## 5. Responsible area and procedure

Requests, enquiries and complaints are handled by the Founder, acting directly as the Data
Controller, at **privacidad@freezehub.io**.

### 5.1 Enquiry

The data subject may enquire about the data held in the databases. An enquiry is answered
within a **maximum of ten (10) business days** from receipt. Where it is not possible to answer
within that period, the enquirer will be informed before it expires, stating the reasons and
the date on which it will be answered, which will in no case exceed **five (5) business days**
after the expiry of the first period.

### 5.2 Complaint

A data subject who believes their data should be corrected, updated or deleted, or who becomes
aware of a suspected breach, may lodge a complaint stating their identification, a description
of the facts, their contact address and any documents they wish to rely on.

If the complaint is incomplete, the complainant will be asked within **five (5) days** to remedy
the deficiencies. If **two (2) months** pass from the date of that request without the required
information being supplied, the complaint will be deemed withdrawn.

A complaint is answered within a **maximum of fifteen (15) business days** counted from the day
after receipt. Where it is not possible to answer within that period, the complainant will be
informed of the reasons for the delay and the date on which it will be answered, which will in
no case exceed **eight (8) business days** after the expiry of the first period.

While a complaint is pending, the data in question will be marked as such in the databases.

### 5.3 Precondition to complaining to the regulator

Under the proviso to article 16 of Ley 1581 de 2012, the data subject may only lodge a
complaint with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio once they have exhausted the
enquiry or complaint procedure with Camilo Andres Hurtado Carvajal.

## 6. International transmission

The service's infrastructure is hosted on **Amazon Web Services, region `us-east-2` (United
States)**. This constitutes a **transmission** of personal data — not a transfer — since the
recipient acts as Processor on the Controller's behalf, and it is governed by article 25 of
Decreto 1377 de 2013 by means of a contract stipulating the scope, the purposes, the security
and confidentiality obligations and the applicable instructions.

The Processors that receive personal data, and what each one receives, are published and kept
up to date in `legal/subprocessors.md`. They include **Google LLC** (United States), whose
calendar is offered to anyone requesting a demonstration.

## 7. Security measures

Camilo Andres Hurtado Carvajal adopts technical and administrative measures to seek to secure
the data and prevent its alteration, loss, or unauthorized consultation, use or access. These
include:

- encryption in transit and at rest, including encryption of stored secret values;
- isolation of each customer organization's data;
- identity-based access control, with short-lived credentials for administrative operations;
- an audit record of operations performed on the data;
- periodic backups.

No measure eliminates risk entirely, and this document does not claim otherwise.

## 8. Incident handling

In the event of a security incident affecting personal data, Camilo Andres Hurtado Carvajal
contains it, assesses it and, when acting as Processor, informs each affected Controller
organization without undue delay. When acting as Controller, it informs the Superintendencia de
Industria y Comercio on the applicable terms. Every incident is recorded, whether or not it is
notifiable.

## 9. Term and amendments

This Policy is in force from 5 October 2026. The databases will be retained for the periods set
out in section 2.1, or for as long as the purpose that justified their collection and the legal
retention duties subsist, if that period is shorter.

Any substantial amendment will be communicated to data subjects before it takes effect, through
the contact channel they have provided.
