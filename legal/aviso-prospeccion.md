# Aviso de prospección

The block that goes in the footer of a **cold outreach** email, and nowhere else.

It is not the Aviso de Privacidad. That one (`aviso-de-privacidad.md`) is Decreto 1377
Art. 14's short form, shown at the moment somebody hands over their data. Here nobody hands
over anything: the data was obtained from a public source, the Política relies on Ley 1581
Art. 10 for that, and **Art. 10 removes the authorization, not the duty to inform** under
Art. 12. The first message is the only moment that duty can be discharged, so it is
discharged in the footer.

See `politica-de-tratamiento.md` § 2.1 for the basis and its two stated limits.

## Why each line is there, so none of them is cut as clutter

| Line | What requires it |
|---|---|
| Why you are receiving this | Art. 12(a) — the finalidad, and the only honest answer to "how did you get my address" |
| The public-source statement | The Art. 10 basis is only available for data of a public nature; claiming it silently is worse than claiming it badly |
| Controller identity, NIT, physical address | Decreto 1377 Art. 13(a). The same details the Política carries, because a recipient should not have to click to learn who is writing |
| Link to the Política | Art. 12(c) and Art. 14 — where the full terms can be read |
| `privacidad@freezehub.io` | Art. 13(d) — the channel where a consulta or reclamo arrives, which starts the statutory clock |
| Opt-out *and* deletion | Art. 8(e). "Reply no" stops the mail; it does not delete the record, and the two are different rights |

## What this block does not do

**It does not make the send lawful everywhere.** It addresses the Colombian obligation to
inform. The rules on whether a commercial message may be *sent at all* belong to the
recipient's country, are separate from whether the data may be processed here, and in at
least one jurisdiction on the current list are stricter: Germany's UWG § 7 treats unsolicited
B2B advertising email as unlawful without prior consent. No footer cures that.

**It is not a substitute for counsel.** `OI-51` names Colombian counsel as a blocker on all of
these documents. The treatment of a *work email address* as a dato de naturaleza pública is
the load-bearing claim in this one, and it is the first thing a lawyer should be asked about.
